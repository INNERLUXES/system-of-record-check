# A messy map

A made-up integration map of a made-up wholesaler, Fernbrook Supply, written in YAML the way a map often looks after a
few years of projects. It holds one problem of most kinds: a customer claimed by both the CRM and an old CRM, a price
claimed by both the ERP and the web shop, a product and a shipping address that no system owns, a credit limit synced
both ways, an order number passed on by a system that does not own it, an email address written back into the CRM
under an exception with no reason, copies that no flow from the owner feeds, a flow to a system the map does not
describe, a flow of a field its target does not hold, and two systems with no team or no contact.
